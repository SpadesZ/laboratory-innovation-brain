  • Position: sp.specialist.semiconductor_device: The device physics indicates that a depletion effect cannot explain a 50% difference in series resistance (Rs) with indistinguishable junction capacitance (Cj) between splits A and B. The constant offset in Rs across reverse bias points to a bias-independent resistance, such as contact resistance. However, the extraction log indicates that the contact-resistance correction term was unavailable, meaning the observed difference could either be a genuine physical process-dependent contact resistance or an artefact of the missing correction in the extraction script. A simulation mesh artefact is disfavoured as it would perturb the capacitance non-uniformly.
