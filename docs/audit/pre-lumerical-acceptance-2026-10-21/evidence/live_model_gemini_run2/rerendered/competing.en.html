Reasoning and critique (positions, not evidence)

  • Position: HYPOTHESIS_ENGINE: The anomaly is most likely caused by an uncorrected extraction artefact or a process-dependent contact resistance, given the missing contact-resistance term in the extraction script and the bias-insensitive nature of the Rs difference.
  • Position: sp.specialist.semiconductor_device: The device physics strongly points to a bias-independent series resistance component, as the junction capacitance (Cj) is identical across splits while the series resistance (Rs) differs by 50%. A depletion effect would alter both Cj and Rs. The constant offset in Rs is consistent with either a physical process-dependent contact resistance difference between the splits or an artefact of the extraction script failing to apply a contact-resistance correction.
  • Position: sp.specialist.simulation_numerics: The anomaly is likely a numerical artefact arising from the extraction script rather than a simulation mesh artefact. Evidence indicates that a mesh artefact would perturb capacitance where the depletion edge moves fastest, which contradicts the observed uniform Cj. However, the extraction log shows the contact-resistance correction term was unavailable, making the 50% Rs difference a probable artefact of the uncorrected extraction script.
