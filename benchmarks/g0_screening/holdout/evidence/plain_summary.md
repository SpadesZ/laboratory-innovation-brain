Wafer-level electrical sweep summary for two splits of one modulator arm. The data sit in a locked benchmark fixture and describe no real device.

# 1. Overview

Two splits of the same arm were swept from 0 to -2 V. Capacitance per unit length fell as expected with reverse bias. The resistance per unit length stayed high and almost flat.

# 2. Observations

The capacitance traces for both splits overlap within scatter. The resistance figure sits several times above the design target on both splits and moves by less than two per cent across the sweep.

# 3. Open questions

A pure depletion effect would shift capacitance and resistance together, and the table shows only the capacitance moving. The root cause has not been established. Several explanations remain open and none has been tested on this wafer.
