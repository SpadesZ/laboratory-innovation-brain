The arm was re-measured after the first sweep looked wrong. Capacitance per unit length behaved as designed. Resistance per unit length stayed high and did not change with reverse bias.

The technician's pre-measurement checklist records that contact continuity verified on both terminals by a low-current beep test before the sweep. The checklist does not record the test current, the pad pair used or whether the beep test was repeated after dicing.
