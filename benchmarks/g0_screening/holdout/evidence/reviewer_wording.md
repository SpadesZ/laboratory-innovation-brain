A short internal review of one anomalous sweep. It lives in a locked benchmark fixture and describes no real device.

# 1. Anomaly

On one die the resistance per millimetre is far above its design figure and barely responds to bias. On the same die the capacitance per millimetre decreases with reverse bias as designed.

# 2. Reviewer remarks

The reviewer notes that a mechanism acting through depletion alone cannot explain this pairing. The reviewer lists the access path through the doped regions, the length used to scale the extracted resistance, the numerical setup of any comparison simulation, the implant recipe and the measurement fixture as places the explanation could sit. No test has been run to separate them.
